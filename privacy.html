OffGrid Comms — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Aetheria Continuum UK

1. Zero Servers & Zero Data Collection

OffGrid Comms operates 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell personal data, analytics, phone numbers, email addresses, or user accounts on any external server.

2. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) Mesh

Bluetooth permissions are used strictly to discover nearby OffGrid Comms devices and relay text messages and optional GPS pins peer-to-peer. Bluetooth scanning is never used to track your physical location.

3. Optional GPS Pitch Compass

Satellite GPS coordinates are processed locally on your device to calculate distance and bearing to your saved campsite pitch, and are only shared over Bluetooth when you explicitly tap "Drop Pitch Pin" or "SOS Alert".

4. On-Device Encryption & Storage

Messages in the Family Vault are encrypted on-device using AES-GCM-256 before transmission. All chat history remains stored locally on your device until you clear it.
